What happens on submit

  • The project becomes visible in the public gallery.
  • The organizer can assign judges to it (round-robin within its track).
  • Only its own team members can still edit it, and only until the deadline.

Rules enforced by the database

  • One project per team per event — UNIQUE(event_id, team_id).
  • One team per participant per event — UNIQUE(event_id, user_id) on team membership.
  • Deadline checks run server-side against UTC.
